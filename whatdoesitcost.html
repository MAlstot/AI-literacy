Notice: pick "Make images" and watch the bar. Image generation is not a little more expensive than text. It is about 1,450 times the cost of sorting, and about 46 times the next-highest task.
But what about training the model? Isn't that the real cost?

It is an enormous cost, and it happens once. Training GPT-3 used about 1,287 megawatt-hours. Training BLOOM used 433, and Gopher 1,066.

Here is the part that surprises people. BLOOM used more energy in training than OPT (433 versus 324 MWh) but produced far less carbon (25 versus 70 tonnes), because it was trained on a cleaner electricity grid. Where the electricity comes from matters as much as how much you use.

Your individual prompt is tiny next to training. But training happens once and prompting happens billions of times a day, which is why both numbers are real and neither one settles the question by itself.

Does this mean I should not use AI?

No. It means decide before you generate.

Sketch what you want first, then ask. Explore at a lower quality setting and save the high-quality run for the version you are actually going to use. Ask for four images because you thought about it, instead of two hundred because you were scrolling until something felt right.

Nothing on this page tells you what to conclude. It tells you what your choices cost, so the deciding is yours.
