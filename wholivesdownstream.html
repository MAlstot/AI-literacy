Who Lives Downstream?

Two data centers have been proposed near Waco. Here is who is standing near them.

Locations approximate, for orientation rather than navigation. Both were still proposals as of mid-2026, and this story moves.

Site 1 · Elm Mott / Lacy Lakeview

$10 billion · 520 acres · developer: Infrakey

Includes an on-site gas power plant rated at 1.2 gigawatts, enough for roughly 300,000 homes. That would make it larger than any power plant currently operating in McLennan County.

Site 2 · near Riesel

About 300 acres · developer: Cipher Digital

Could draw up to 500 megawatts, the power roughly 100,000 Texas homes use on a peak summer day. How much water it will need has not been disclosed.

What this page is and is not

Data centers carry both effects. They require land, electricity, and water, and they bring construction activity, infrastructure investment, and tax revenue that funds city services. Which of those matters most depends on where you are standing, which is why this page shows several positions rather than one.

Several of the questions raised here have no settled answer anywhere: what a company owes a community it is not buying land from, how much resource use it should disclose and when, and who should pay for the infrastructure a facility needs. Law and practice are still forming around all three.

Nothing here argues for or against these projects. They are real proposals in McLennan County, the facts above come from local reporting, and the decisions are being made in public while you are here.
