Prompt Builder

Build a prompt for something you're actually working on this week. Fill in the four parts, check it, then test it in your AI tool.

Private by design. There's no AI on this page. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere, and it disappears when you leave. The tips below are general guidance, not a grade.

1

Pick your task

What are you working on? The tips you get after you check your prompt will match your choice.
2

Build your prompt

What you're working on and what you're trying to accomplish: the class, the topic or assignment, and where you are in it.

Who you want AI to be. The role decides how much of the thinking stays with you.

Exactly what you want AI to do. "Help me" is too vague.

The limits AI must follow. AI won't manage your learning for you. Rails are how you stay in charge of it.

3

Your prompt

Want to change something? Edit the boxes above and select Check my prompt again.

4

Test it

  1. Open Copilot (sign in with your Baylor account) or another AI tool you use, in a new tab.
  2. Start a new chat.
  3. Paste your prompt.
  4. Check what you're about to share. Share your own work and your course's assignment directions and rubric. Remove other people's names or private information. If it isn't yours, like a classmate's work or a professor's slides, ask first.
  5. Give AI the work you're asking about. Attach or paste it with your prompt: your draft, your notes, the rubric and assignment directions, or the problem and your work so far. AI can only respond to what it can see.
  6. Send it, then do your part: answer, try, explain.
  7. If AI goes off track, correct it in the chat. Remember the fixes from A Good Prompt: "Shorter, please." "Stop. Don't show answers until I've tried."
5

Check what came back

Any boxes left unchecked? Revise your prompt above, copy it again, and try it in a new chat. Then notice: what changed in AI's response?
