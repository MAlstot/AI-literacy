Prompt Guide: Keep the Thinking Yours

Prompts that ask AI to support your learning, not replace it, for any class.

You don't need AI to study well. But when you use it, by choice or because a class requires it, you stay in charge of the thinking.

Pick the task you're working on. Copy a prompt, then replace each [bracket] with your own class and assignment details. Check your syllabus for your professor's AI policy first.
